Tankless Water Heater

Do you prefer hot water without the bulkiness of a regular water heater? Tankless water heaters heat water as it passes through the apparatus, providing you with endless hot water! They take up substantially less space in your home and deliver an amplified lifespan compared to most water heaters.

Water Heater Installation and Repair

When you need a new water heater, our experienced contractors can help your family make the best choice for their needs. We have installation options that will meet any size home's unique storage requirements: from small 30-40 gallon units perfect if there are only two people living in it or larger 80+ Gallon models which provide enough space to accommodate everyone's weekly routine! JHenry Plumbing also provides expert repair and maintenance for all your water heater needs.

Another option is the tankless water heater. The tankless water heater is an energy-efficient and cost-effective way to provide hot showers for your family. Unlike storage tanks, these systems heat water as you need it rather than storing it in a large container that will run out eventually!
