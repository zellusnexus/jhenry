What Causes Clogged Drains?

The most common cause of a clogged kitchen sink is food, but you can also see blockages from grease and coffee grounds. These foods and materials become stuck in your sewer line over time. In bathroom drains, a hair clog can be just as damaging.

Signs That You Have A Clogged Drain

Most of the time, you'll be able to tell when you have a clogged drain in your home. Some signs are obvious, but there are also less obvious ones to watch out for. If you notice any of the following, it's time to call a professional plumber:

  • You hear or see bubbling coming from your drain
  • Your toilet will not flush
  • Water is slow to drain
  • The water rises in your shower or tub
  • There are puddles of water around your sinks, showers, tubs or toilets
