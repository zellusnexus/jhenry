Who is J. Henry Plumbing?

We handle all plumbing issues, big or small, with skill and expertise.

We love what we do, and it shows. We've been in the plumbing business for more than 20 years, helping people solve their plumbing issues quickly and professionally. All of our plumbers are licensed professionals who take pride in what they do. We're available for all your plumbing needs, from an irritating tap that leaks to full-scale plumbing installation and maintenance.

Is your kitchen or bathroom tap dripping?

We are dedicated to giving your fast, friendly and reliable service. We offer services every day of the week, at the time that's best for you.
